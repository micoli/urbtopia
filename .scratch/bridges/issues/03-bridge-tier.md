# Road Bridge tier and upgrade

Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01
Spec: ../spec.md

## What to build

A Road Bridge has one Road tier for its three parts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Initial tier is the lower tier of its two ends.
- [ ] Upgrading any tile of the Bridge upgrades the three parts together, at the summed cost, capped like a Road.
- [ ] Its capacity in Congestion follows its tier.
