# Tooltip With Causes

Status: resolved
Spec: ../spec.md
Blocked by: 05, 06, 07

## What to build

Tapping or hovering a tile or Home shows a tooltip with the Layer's figure and its two or three main causes, by plain rules over existing figures, reusing the Diagnosis shape (translation key plus parameters).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Works for every Layer, touch and mouse.
- [ ] Causes pure, ranked by weight, tested.
- [ ] FR/EN texts, no free-text generation.
