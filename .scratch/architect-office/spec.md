# Architect office

Status: ready-for-agent

Decisions: [ADR 0091](../../docs/adr/0091-the-architect-office-turns-a-player-plan-into-a-home-of-the-city.md).

## Confirmed scope

- A new building, the Architect office, opens the studio from its side panel.
- The plans of the player are saved in the city.
- A saved plan can be built as a Home, like any other housing.
- A cell of a plan is a quarter of a tile on each side; a plan is at most 6×6 cells, so a house covers up to 2×2 tiles.

## Tickets

1. The Architect office building opens the studio.
2. Plans in the save.
3. A plan becomes a Home type of the city.
4. The scene draws and the city builds the Homes of plans.
5. Demonstration, guide and codex.
6. Houses of plans look like the Homes of the game.
7. Architect office Tiers gate assets and plan count.
