# A plan becomes a Home type of the city

Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02
Spec: ../spec.md

## What to build

The Home type derived from a saved plan: footprint, Citizens, Demand and price, listed in the Build menu.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The footprint and the cost come from the plan. Citizens and Demand stay those of a Tier 1 Home (a house plan has one Tier): deriving them from the plan is left for a later ticket.
- [x] The Build menu lists the plans in the housing section and builds them like a Home.
- [x] Deleting a plan keeps the Homes already built from it.
