# Progression and unlocks

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 07, 08

## Question

Define the MVP progression curve: levels or population thresholds, what unlocks when, first-session pacing, and the end-of-MVP state.

## Answer

Decided in one grilling round, all recommendations accepted. All numbers are starting values for the later balancing pass.

**Metric.** Total Citizens of the city is the only progression metric. No level, no XP, no objectives list. It is derived from state, nothing extra to save.

**Available from the start (0 Citizens).** Materials Wood and Stone; Goods Planks and Bricks; buildings Workshop, Factory, Shop, Storehouse, Power plant, Water tower, Home. The Market panel opens with the first Good (ticket 07). Everything needed to build the first Home and reach Home tier 3 is open from the start.

**Unlock thresholds (total Citizens).**

| Citizens | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 30 | Clay, Tiles |
| 80 | Metal, Tools |
| 200 | Silicon, Glass, Circuits |

Each threshold opens the Goods before the Home tier that needs them (Tiles before tier 4, Tools before tier 5, Glass and Circuits before tier 6).

**Parcels.** No population gate; the exponential Urbs cost (ticket 08) is the only brake. Reopen at the balancing pass if needed.

**HUD.** Shows only the next threshold ("18/30 Citizens: Clay, Tiles"). First-session pacing relies on ticket 07 targets (first Shop and sales before 5 min, first Home before 10 min). Guided tutorial stays in the fog.

**End state.** No ending, open sandbox, no victory screen. "Complete" means everything unlocked (200 Citizens) and Homes reachable at tier 6. The natural ceiling is the Parcel price curve (64 Parcels total). Next content (services, transport) comes after the MVP.
