# 16: Factory Goods, extra Slots and Storehouse upgrades

**What to build:** The player turns Materials into Goods in a Factory and spends Urbs to grow throughput and storage.

**Blocked by:** 15

**Status:** resolved

- [x] Factory consumes Materials from the Storehouse and produces Planks (2 Wood, 2 min) and Bricks (2 Stone + 1 Wood, 4 min); any Factory produces any unlocked recipe
- [x] Queueing is refused with a clear message if Materials are missing
- [x] Extra Slots purchasable on Workshop and Factory (3rd 500, 4th 1,500, 5th 4,000 Urbs; max 5); not refunded on sale
- [x] Storehouse upgrades (+10 Materials, +20 Goods) cost 300, 800, 2,000, 5,000, 12,000 Urbs; five upgrades max
- [x] Recipes, Slot prices and upgrade prices are data tables
- [x] Core tests cover recipes, shortage, Slot purchase and upgrades
