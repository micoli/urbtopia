## What to build

Final models for the stages of each of the 18 species (four growth stages, ready, and the after-harvest stage where it exists) and Codex entries for the species.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every species maps to its stage models; missing stages fall back to the previous one.
- [ ] Assets come from the existing Kenney kits, extracted through the build script.
- [ ] Codex lists species with their Unlock threshold, growth time, water need and yield.
- [ ] FR/EN strings.
