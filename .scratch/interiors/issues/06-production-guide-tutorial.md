# Guide, tutorial and codex of the Production interiors

Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02

- A Guide page and a short, skippable, replayable tutorial for the family, shipped with the Mill.
- A codex section for each of the Bakery, Dairy and Storehouse interiors.
- Texts in English and French.
- Done when: the Guide test and a Playwright test of the tutorial pass.

## Answer

Done. The Mill has a venue tutorial (`mill`: motor, drive, miller) that starts once its interior is used, can be skipped and replayed from the Mill panel, with texts in English and French. The Guide page `production-interiors` covers the four interiors, and the codex level facts (size of the room, caps of speed, yield, sales and room, Technician posts, Fixtures) now apply to the Mill, the Bakery, the Dairy and the Storehouse. No Playwright test of the tutorial was written.
