# 09: Service vehicles

**What to build:** Ambulances, fire trucks and police cars leave their facilities by road and drive to covered Homes and back, as visual life in the city.

**Blocked by:** 06

**Status:** done

- [ ] Models are `ambulance`, `firetruck` and `police` from `assets/kenney/kenney_car-kit.zip`.
- [ ] Each Hospital, Fire station and Police station makes one round trip every 30–60 real seconds to a random covered Home, chosen through the Seed.
- [ ] Each facility has at most one Service vehicle at a time.
- [ ] A vehicle appears only when a road connects the facility to the Home.
- [ ] Service vehicles have no gameplay effect and are not saved.
