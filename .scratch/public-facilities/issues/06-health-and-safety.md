# 06: Health and Safety facilities

**What to build:** The player can build a Hospital, a Fire station and a Police station, which gate Home Tiers 5 and 6.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** done

- [ ] Hospital: Unlock at 100 Citizens, 1 500 Urbs, radius 14, capacity 800, 3×3, with electricity and water Demand.
- [ ] Fire station and Police station: Unlock at 160 Citizens, 1 000 Urbs, radius 12, capacity 800, 2×2, with electricity Demand only.
- [ ] Gates: Tier 5 requires a Hospital; Tier 6 requires a Fire station and a Police station.
- [ ] Each facility has a model, Codex entry, Unlock notification and save support.
