# 07: Culture

**What to build:** The player can build a Community hall, a Theater and a Concert hall. Different Culture facilities stack Well-being with diminishing returns. Culture is optional and never gates a Home Tier.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** done

- [ ] Community hall: Unlock at 32 Citizens, 150 Urbs, radius 6, capacity 150, 1×1.
- [ ] Theater: Unlock at 250 Citizens, 800 Urbs, radius 10, capacity 500, 2×2.
- [ ] Concert hall: Unlock at 600 Citizens, 2 000 Urbs, radius 14, capacity 1 200, 3×3.
- [ ] Coverage by different Culture facility types stacks with diminishing returns.
- [ ] Each facility has a model, Codex entry, Unlock notification and save support.
