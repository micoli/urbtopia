# 10: Facility evolutions

**What to build:** Public facilities with a limited capacity evolve through eight Tiers that raise their capacity, so one building can serve more Homes.

**Blocked by:** 01

**Status:** done

- [ ] The School starts at a capacity of 300 Citizens.
- [ ] Tiers 1 to 8 multiply capacity by 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5.5, 7 and 9.
- [ ] Upgrading costs Urbs only: 60 %, 120 %, 240 %, 400 %, 600 %, 900 % and 1300 % of the construction cost.
- [ ] The Town hall, with unlimited capacity, does not evolve; Universities evolve and add their capacities.
- [ ] The selection panel shows Tier, reach, capacity, served Citizens and the upgrade action.
- [ ] The Codex shows eight levels per facility.
