# 04: Complete Education

**What to build:** The player can build a Middle school, a High school and a University, which gate Home Tiers 4, 5 and 7.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** done

- [ ] Middle school: Unlock at 60 Citizens, 600 Urbs, radius 10, capacity 400, 2×2.
- [ ] High school: Unlock at 100 Citizens, 1 000 Urbs, radius 12, capacity 600, 3×2.
- [ ] University: Unlock at 400 Citizens, 3 000 Urbs, covers the whole city, capacity 2 000, 3×3; multiple Universities add their capacity.
- [ ] Gates: Tier 4 requires a Middle school, Tier 5 a High school and Tier 7 a University.
- [ ] Each facility has a model, Codex entry, Unlock notification and save support.
