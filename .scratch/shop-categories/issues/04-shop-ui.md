# Shop panel and upgrade UI

Status: ready-for-agent
Spec: [shop-categories](../spec.md)

Show the Shop's Good category and Tier in its side panel, the Goods it can sell at this Tier (and which unlock at the next), the Tier upgrade action and cost, and restrict the stack Good picker to the sellable Goods. Text in en and fr.

Acceptance:
- The Good picker lists only sellable Goods.
