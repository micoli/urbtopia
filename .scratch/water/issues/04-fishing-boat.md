# Fishing boat, Fish and Canned fish

Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02
Spec: ../spec.md

## What to build

A Fishing boat produces Fish over time, stored in the Storehouse; a Factory turns Fish into Canned fish.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Fishing boat: 800 Urbs, unlocked at 100 Citizens, placed like a Pleasure boat; fixed duration and yield per cycle, Slots from the Marina Tier.
- [ ] `Fish` Material and `Canned fish` Good with a Factory recipe, wired in the economy tables and unlocks.
- [ ] Production runs in Catch-up (up to 48 game hours), no Jobs and no Commute.
- [ ] FR/EN strings and item names; tests in `src/core/economy`.
