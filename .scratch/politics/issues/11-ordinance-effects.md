# Ordinance effects

Status: needs-triage
Blocked by: 04

Apply the effect of every adopted Ordinance to the systems it names: Tax multiplier on Homes, Waste reduction, Well-being bonus of the green belt, Congestion relief, power Demand reduction, Office yield, Esteem bonus. The effect values come from the Ordinance data; the rules that read them stay in code, in one module.

Acceptance: each effect changes exactly the system it names and nothing else; two Ordinances of one exclusive group never stack; an effect ends the moment its Ordinance is repealed or lapses; tests per effect kind.
