Status: done - implemented
Blocked by: 02

## What to build

A section of the Social window listing the top ten: rank, Avatar, Handle, Citizens, and a "Visit" button. Open to anonymous accounts (list only, no Friends tabs).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Fetched on open and then polled like the other Social panels (pause while the tab is hidden, ADR 0026).
- [ ] Empty, loading and offline states; own entry highlighted.
- [ ] No Medals, no add-Friend button.
- [ ] FR/EN strings, component tests and a story.
