## The ten biggest cities

The **Hall of fame** ranks the ten biggest cities by Citizens, one city per Player account. Your own city is never listed. Each entry shows the Avatar, the Handle and the Citizens count. It lives in the Social window, and anyone can read it, even without an email.

## Taking part

Every Player account with a Handle is in the Hall of fame by default. The first time you open the game after the Hall of fame arrives, a notice asks whether to keep your city in; nothing is published before you answer. You can change your mind at any time with the **Appear in the Hall of fame** switch in your profile: turning it off removes your city at once.

Your **Showcase** is the copy of your city that other players see. It is separate from your Cloud save, which stays private, and it is replaced each time your city is saved online. It does not include your trade history.

## Visiting a city

Press **Visit** on an entry to look around that city. A Visit is read-only: time stands still, nothing can be built or changed, and your own city and saves are never touched. A banner names the city you are visiting; **Back to my city** returns you exactly where you were.

If a city was saved by a newer version of the game, reload the page to update before visiting it.

## A friendly ranking

The Citizens count is reported by each player’s game. To keep the ranking honest, the server holds back a count that grows implausibly fast, and a Block hides two players from each other. A rank carries no reward.
