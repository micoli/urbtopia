## When Waste starts to matter

Every Home produces Waste each day, in proportion to its Citizens. It only matters once the city has about 1,000 Citizens; a city that is already past that size gets an **Adaptation period** before Waste counts.

Waste is a Service, like education or health: a Home whose Waste is not processed lacks Waste coverage. That lowers its Well-being, can stop it from reaching a higher Tier, and costs Tax from Easy Difficulty.

## Three facilities

| Facility | Capacity | Drawbacks | Cost per unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <EntryLink id="recyclingPlant" /> | Medium, widest reach | Needs power | Highest |
| <EntryLink id="incinerator" /> | Large, in a small footprint | Lowers nearby Well-being, adds emissions | Medium |
| <EntryLink id="mound" /> | Small: build many side by side | Its odor lowers Well-being farther than it collects | Lowest |

A Mound never fills up: it handles a daily amount, forever. The odor of several Mounds never adds up; a Home bears the worst one around it, so a landfill is a matter of where you put it.

## Collection needs a road

A Home is covered when a Waste facility **reaches** it, has **capacity** left, and is **joined to it by road**, since trucks have to get there. A BRT corridor counts as a road for this link: a Home beside one is joined to the roads the corridor touches or crosses. A Home reached only by Pedestrian ways is joined when its paths touch a linked road close enough, at a higher cost. When a Home is not covered, its panel says why: out of reach, capacity full, or no road link.

## Processing order

Each day, Waste goes to the cleanest facilities first: Recycling plants, then Incinerators, then Mounds, whatever they cost. You choose the mix by what you build, not with a setting. Each unit processed costs Urbs; a facility you cannot pay stops, and its Homes lose their coverage.

## Understanding a shortage

When Homes lack Waste coverage, the Waste section of the City management window explains why in a few lines, heaviest first, each with a button to show the place on the map:

- **No facility** at all: build one, and a Road to it.
- **Capacity short**: the city produces more than its facilities can process. Add facilities.
- **Facility stopped**: no Urbs left to pay it, no road link, or no power for a Recycling plant.
- **No road link**: Homes in reach of a facility but joined to it by no road.
- **Out of reach**: Homes too far from every facility, or too far from its road along the Pedestrian paths.
- **Pedestrian surcharge**: Homes served only by Pedestrian ways cost more to collect. Give them a Road.
- **Odor and smoke**: Mounds and Incinerators lower the Well-being of the Homes around them.
