## What it is

If you are unhappy with the layout of your city, **Replan** it. Open City management, then **Cities**, and press **Replan** on the row of the open city. The window closes: **draw a rectangle** on the map with two opposite corners. Everything you placed inside it, on every level (buildings, roads of every kind, water, fields, orchard trees, hives, underground), goes into the **Stash**, and you lay it out again where you want. The rest of the city stays as it is. A building the rectangle only touches comes out whole.

Your land does not change, and neither do your Urbs, your Citizens, your objectives or your politics. A building comes back with its Tier and its interior: a Venue keeps its fixtures, a Factory its Factory interior, a Home its furniture.

## While it lasts

- The game **stands still**: nothing is produced, earned or judged, and the Day cycle pauses. When you finish, the time you spent is not caught up.
- It is **free**: no Urbs are spent or earned, and Build limits and prices do not move.
- Only the layout can change. Everything else waits, apart from trade offers and donations.
- You can close the game and come back: the Replan is saved, and carried by your Cloud save.

## Placing things again

The Stash lists what is left, grouped by type. Pick a group, then place it on the map. Buildings, bridges and boats stay in hand so you can place the next one; roads, pedestrian ways and walkways are drawn from a start to an end like new ones, and water and fields are painted.

Placing is only checked for what the object needs on its own: free tiles and your own land. You can put a house down before its road. A bridge still needs its water, and a boat the water around its marina.

## Transit lines

Bus and transit **lines stay**. Only the stops that went into the Stash are taken out of them, and a stop you place again is not put back in its line: add it yourself. A line left with fewer than two stops disappears, and its vehicles wait unassigned.

## Finishing or cancelling

- **Finish** is open when the Stash is empty. A summary lists the buildings with no road, BRT corridor or pedestrian way in front of them, and lists the transit lines that lost a stop. It does not stop you.
- **Cancel Replan** puts your city back exactly as it was when you started, lines included.

You can also **drop** a group of the Stash with a long press: it pays back its initial price, tiers and options included, and Cancel takes it back.

A Replan cannot start during a Challenge, during the tutorial, or while a Save conflict is open.
