## Gatherings

An **Arena** earns only from its **Gatherings**: one-day crowd events that bring Tourists and Citizens to the building. There are three kinds, and they differ by who sets them and where they are held.

| Kind | Where | Who sets it | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Show** | Theater, Concert hall, Colosseum, Pirate cove | You, when you want | Urbs |
| **Match** | Football stadium or baseball field with a Club | The Club's Championship | None |
| **Meeting** | Any stadium with no Club | The calendar, once a day | None |

## Shows

You programme a Show from the building's panel, now or up to 24 hours ahead. It lasts three hours, and the Arena then needs 12 hours of rest. Cancelled before it starts, it gives back half of its cost. The crowd depends on the seats of the building, the Ticket, the Well-being of the city and the people living nearby. The Concert hall adds an **Artist**, and the Pirate cove adds its ships and its Stands. See the <ArticleLink slug="tourism">Tourism</ArticleLink> page for the details.

## Matches

A Match is held in a stadium that hosts a **Club**, and the Championship sets when. You play it yourself or let the lineup play it, and it fills the whole stadium. The better the Club does, the bigger the crowd of the next Match. See the <ArticleLink slug="clubs">Clubs and Matches</ArticleLink> page.

## Meetings

A stadium with no Club still holds a **Meeting** every day, for free. It is set by the calendar, has no result and no ranking, and seats the same crowd whatever the stadium, so that only a Match fills it.

## The four phases

Every Gathering goes through the same four phases: scheduled, the crowd arriving in the hour before, under way, and the crowd leaving in the hour after, when the Ticket sales are paid into the Takings. While the crowd moves, those who drive load the network and weigh a little on the Well-being around the building.

## Not Tourist events

Two other occasions share the word event, but bring no crowd to an Arena. A **Challenge** is a server event that lasts several days and pays Medals. An **Annual event**, such as Halloween or Midsummer, is tied to the calendar and opens cosmetic items to build for a few weeks.
