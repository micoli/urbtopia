## What they are

The **School**, the **Library** and the **Hospital** open an interior: a room where you place furniture. It earns no Takings and has no Visitors. An empty interior leaves the facility exactly as it was. A furnished one adds **quality** to the service, up to a cap that rises with the facility's Tier. These interiors are a beta feature: switch on *Service interiors* in the Settings, and your city needs enough Citizens for them to open.

Furniture never wears, and the reach and the capacity of the facility do not change: the interior only makes the service better for the Homes it already serves.

## Furniture adds quality

Every piece adds a little quality. The first pieces count in full, then each adds less, and the total never reaches the cap of the Tier. A **blackboard** hangs on the back wall of the room, like a painting in a Home.

## Staff deliver it

Furniture alone does nothing: someone has to use it. Each facility hires its own roles, like the Staff of a Venue (a fee of five days of wages once, then a wage by the day), and the quality only counts in proportion to the Staff who actually come to work.

- A **teacher** raises the Schooling a School gives to the Knowledge of the Homes it serves.
- A **librarian** raises the Knowledge a Library gives.
- At the Hospital, a **nurse**, a **doctor** and a **surgeon** each deliver a part of the care, which adds Well-being to the Homes it serves. The nurse comes first, the doctor from Tier 2 and the surgeon from Tier 3.

## What it costs

A facility has no running cost of its own, but the Staff you hire are paid every hour, as far as your Urbs go, and fill Jobs of the building. A facility with no interior, or no Staff hired, costs nothing, as before.
