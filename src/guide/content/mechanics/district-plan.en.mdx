## What it is

The **Planning office** hosts a short placement game, the **District plan**. It opens from the side panel of the office, on a screen of its own: the city stands still while you play, and nothing in it changes until the game ends with a better score than before. You can play as often as you like.

## How a game goes

A game is a **Deck** of **Hexes**: 24 at Tier 1, and more at each Tier of the office (28, 32, 36, 40). Each Hex has six edges, and each edge has a category: **Habitat**, **Park**, **Commerce**, **Industry**, **Service** or **Water**. A Hex mixes two categories.

Every game of an office plays the **same Deck**: a better score comes from better placements, not from a better draw. A Deck of a higher Tier starts with the Hexes of the shorter one.

The first Hex of the Deck, the **Start hex**, is already at the centre of the board. For each other Hex:

1. **Touch a free cell** next to a Hex already placed, within four cells of the centre. A ghost shows the Hex there, and the score it would earn.
2. **Turn** the Hex, one step of 60° at a time, until its edges fit.
3. **Place** it. There is no undo: the ghost is there so that you can look first.

You see the Hex in hand and the next two. The game ends when the Deck is empty. You can give up a game at any time: your best score stays.

## Scoring

Every pair of edges that touch scores. An edge that touches nothing counts for nothing.

| Pair | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Habitat – Park | +3 |
| Habitat – Service | +2 |
| Habitat – Commerce | +1 |
| Habitat – Industry | −2 |
| Park – Water | +2 |
| Commerce – Service | +1 |
| Industry – Water | −1 |
| Two edges of the same category | +1 |
| Any other pair | 0 |

The pairs follow the Well-being of a real city: Habitat likes Parks and Services and does not like Industry.

## Rank and bonus

The best score of an office gives it a **Rank**, by points per Hex of the Deck: **Bronze** from 1.5, **Silver** from 3, **Gold** from 4. A longer Deck asks for more points, so the Rank stays as hard to reach.

The Rank gives the **Homes within reach** of the office a **Well-being bonus**: 40 % of the cap of the Tier for Bronze, 70 % for Silver, 100 % for Gold. The cap and the reach grow with the Tier of the office. A Home takes the **best** bonus among the offices that reach it: they never add up. An office that was never played gives nothing, and never takes anything away.

If you demolish an office, its bonus and its games go with it. If you upgrade it during a game, the game keeps the Deck it started with; the new cap counts at once for your best score, and the next game uses the new Deck.

## Learning it

Press **How to play** on the District plan screen for a short demonstration: six Hexes, and why each one goes where it does.
