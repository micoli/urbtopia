## What it is

Open your <EntryLink id="factory">Factory</EntryLink>, then **Open the Factory floor**. It is a room where you place Fixtures. It earns no Takings and has no Visitors: it only adds a **bonus** to the speed and to the yield of the Factory's Slots. An empty floor changes nothing, so the Factory keeps working as it always did.

## Drive: from a Motor to the Machines

A **Motor** sends a turning force, the **Drive**. **Shafts** carry it in a straight line, **Gears** pass it on, and a **Bevel gear** (elbow or tee) turns it a quarter turn or splits it three ways, so one Motor can serve several Machines. A Machine only takes the Drive if its input faces the line.

- Two Gears meshed rim to rim turn in opposite directions. Gears joined axle to axle turn the same way.
- Some Machines need a given direction (a lathe turns clockwise or counter-clockwise): add or remove a Gear to change it.
- Gears fed in two opposite directions jam, and nothing in the group turns.
- A Motor drives at most **three** Machines and Pumps. The ones past that are overloaded: they stop and show red until you add a Motor.

## Flow: Tank, Pump and Pipes

Some Machines need a fluid. A **Tank** holds it, **Pipes** carry it, and a **Pump** pushes it: the fluid enters at the back of the Pump and leaves by its front. A Pump only works when a Motor drives it, and a Tank with no Pump gives nothing. A Machine that needs both (the Boiler) takes Drive and Flow. One Tank feeds at most **three** Cooling vats: the others get no Flow until you add a Tank.

## What a floor costs and brings

- Each Motor draws power from the city's electricity Demand, a fifth of it when it drives nothing.
- Every **linked** Machine adds its own speed and yield. The first Machines count in full, then each adds less, up to a cap that rises with the Factory's Tier.
- Each different **kind** of Machine that runs adds a little yield: variety pays.
- A **Cooling vat** that is linked and stands beside a Motor raises the speed and the yield of every Machine that Motor drives. A **Boiler** on the same circuit of Flow doubles that.
- Every **Boiler** that runs also saves a quarter of the Motors' electricity, up to half.

## Wear and Repair

Fixtures that run lose Condition hour after hour, and a worn one breaks: everything downstream of it stops until you **Repair** it. Bars above the Fixtures show the wear from 90 %. Hire **Technicians** from Staff to repair for you.
