An **Achievement** is a feature you have used at least once in a City: your first Bus line, your first Engine room, your first Replan. It is yours for good: demolishing what earned it does not take it back. Achievements pay no Urbs; they are there to push you to try every corner of the game.

## Categories and Medals

Achievements are grouped in **categories**. When you have every Achievement of a category, you earn its **Medal**, which shows on your profile in the Social window and on your Friends' lists. A Medal belongs to your account, once per category, whichever City earns it first. Earning one needs an account with a Handle and an internet connection; it is sent as soon as both are there.

## Per City

Achievements are kept in the save of each City, so a new City has its own to earn. When a game saved before Achievements existed is opened, the ones its City already proves are filled in at once.

## What counts

Some Achievements depend on the Difficulty (the Politics category starts at Easy and is absent under Cozy), on a beta feature you have to switch on, or on the online services. Those you cannot earn are left out of the count, so they never keep you from finishing a category. Nothing is earned while you Visit another City or while a Replan is under way, and the Replan Achievement is earned when you finish it.
