## Founding a Club

Select a football stadium and found its **Club**. It starts with a Squad of eighteen players and a budget. The stadium gives the Club a share of the Takings you collect, and the Club pays its Squad a wage every day. A Club that runs into debt sells its best players, but it never goes bankrupt.

## Squad, lineup and recruitment

Open the management page to see the Squad, choose a formation and a tactic, and pick the eleven starters (or let the game pick the best eleven). Once a year new players come onto the market: sign them for a fee, or release the ones you no longer need.

## The Championship

A Championship is ten Matches against seven invented clubs, with a ranking at the end and a prize that goes into the Club budget. Each Match is also a Gathering: it draws a crowd to the stadium. The better the Club does, the more its Fame grows, and a good finish draws extra Tourists for a while.

## Playing a Match

When a Match has started, open the Match tab and play it minute by minute. You can change the tactic and make up to three substitutions as it goes, then record your decisions; if you do nothing, the Match is played with your lineup.
