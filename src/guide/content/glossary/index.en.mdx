<dl className="guide-glossary">
  <dt>Adaptation period</dt>
  <dd>A grace period announced when a new rule starts to apply: meanwhile, power shortages and missing services cost nothing.</dd>
  <dt>Approval</dt>
  <dd>How much the city likes its Mayor. It follows the Well-being of the Homes and reacts to your decisions.</dd>
  <dt>Build limit</dt>
  <dd>How many copies of a building you may own at once. It rises with the number of Citizens.</dd>
  <dt>Citizen</dt>
  <dd>A resident of a Home. Citizens pay Tax, need power and water, and unlock new buildings as their number grows.</dd>
  <dt>Council</dt>
  <dd>The assembly that votes on the Ordinances you propose, from Easy Difficulty once a Town hall is built.</dd>
  <dt>Crop</dt>
  <dd>A plant sown on a Field tile with a Seed pack. It grows through four stages before it can be harvested.</dd>
  <dt>Day cycle</dt>
  <dd>The alternation of Daytime (16 hours) and Night (8 hours) on the map, repeating every 2 real hours. It sets the light and the output of solar and water power.</dd>
  <dt>Demand</dt>
  <dd>The power and water the city needs. Power Demand can exceed production, which cuts service; water Demand cannot.</dd>
  <dt>Difficulty</dt>
  <dd>The setting of a city that decides which missing services reduce Tax: Cozy, Easy, Medium or Hard.</dd>
  <dt>Donation</dt>
  <dd>A gift of Goods to an association, for nothing in return, which fills the city's Solidarity reserve.</dd>
  <dt>Good</dt>
  <dd>An item made by a Factory from Materials, sold in Shops or to the Market.</dd>
  <dt>Home</dt>
  <dd>A residential building with eight Tiers. It upgrades by consuming Goods and houses more Citizens at each Tier.</dd>
  <dt>Market</dt>
  <dd>The buyer of last resort: it buys your Goods for Urbs at any time.</dd>
  <dt>Material</dt>
  <dd>A raw resource made by a Workshop or harvested from a Crop.</dd>
  <dt>Ordinance</dt>
  <dd>A city-wide policy with a cost and an effect, adopted by a vote of the Council.</dd>
  <dt>Parcel</dt>
  <dd>A square of 16 × 16 tiles of land. Buildings must fit inside the Parcels you own; you can buy neighbouring ones.</dd>
  <dt>Pedestrian way</dt>
  <dd>A path for people on foot only, joined to the sidewalks of a Road.</dd>
  <dt>Sanction</dt>
  <dd>A reduction of a Home's Tax caused by a missing service. It never goes below half the Tax.</dd>
  <dt>Seed pack</dt>
  <dd>The seeds needed to sow one Field tile, bought with Urbs and kept in a Farm. Part of each harvest comes back as Seed packs.</dd>
  <dt>Tax</dt>
  <dd>The Urbs your Citizens pay over time, collected by hand from each Home.</dd>
  <dt>Tier</dt>
  <dd>The level of a building. A higher Tier makes it larger and more useful.</dd>
  <dt>Unlock</dt>
  <dd>The moment a building, Material or Good becomes available, when the city reaches its number of Citizens.</dd>
  <dt>Urbs</dt>
  <dd>The currency of the game, earned by selling and collecting, spent on building and upgrading.</dd>
  <dt>Waste</dt>
  <dd>What Citizens throw away every day. It matters from about 1,000 Citizens, when Homes need a Waste facility joined to them by road.</dd>
  <dt>Well-being</dt>
  <dd>The satisfaction of a Home's Citizens, raised by services, green spaces and smooth commutes. It raises Tax.</dd>
</dl>
