# The Livestock farm is a Workshop fed by Crops, apart from the Farm

The Farm stays what it is: the seed stock and the entry point of cultivation, producing nothing. Animal husbandry is a separate Production building, the Livestock farm, built like a Workshop: a queue of Slots, Tiers, Jobs, electricity and a road. Each animal species is a recipe that makes one Animal Material (Eggs, Milk, Wool, Silk) in a fixed time.

Each cycle consumes one Feed taken from the Grain silo: Corn for poultry, Wheat for ruminants, Mulberry leaves for Silkworms. Without Feed the Slot waits, as a Mill waits without Wheat. Species unlock by Citizens count, not by Tier.

No part of the game is made from the death of an animal: no meat, hide or leather. Animals only give what they yield alive.

## Why

- Cultivation and husbandry are two trades. Adding animals to the Farm would turn a stock-holding building into a producer and blur the Farm's glossary definition.
- Feed ties the new chain to the existing one: Crops now have a use beyond Flour, Olive oil and packing, and the Grain silo matters for more than Harvest.
- Reusing the Workshop model, with recipes and Slots, avoids a second production system next to the Orchard tree's passive harvest.

## Consequences

- The Farm's definition, Tiers and Build limit do not change. The Livestock farm has its own Build limit.
- Animal Materials are kept in the Storehouse, not in the Grain silo, which holds Crop Materials only. Mulberry leaves are the exception: they are an Orchard tree harvest and go to the Grain silo with the other fruits.
- Raw Eggs, Milk and Honey are sold through the Packhouse, which gains these inputs. The Dairy and the Knitwear factory sell their own Goods over a Counter, and every Good can also be stocked on a Supermarket Shelf.
- The Hive is not part of the Livestock farm: it is a free-standing perennial placed like an Orchard tree, because it needs no road, no water and no Slots.
- A Livestock farm with no Feed in the Grain silo produces nothing, so a city that stops growing Corn or Wheat stops its eggs and milk too.

## Alternatives rejected

- Extend the Farm with animals: mixes seed stock and production in one building and makes the Farm a producer for the first time.
- One building per species (henhouse, sheepfold, cowshed): many near-identical entries in the build menu, where recipes in Slots already give the same variety.
- Animals that need no Feed: simpler, but the chain would not depend on Crops and the Grain silo.
- Hives as a recipe of the Livestock farm: forces a road, Jobs and power on an object that is naturally scattered over free land.
