# Knowledge rewards everywhere and is sanctioned only on Hard

Education used to be a binary coverage with no effect beyond Well-being and the School required at Tier 3. We add Knowledge, a derived value per Home (0 to 100) built from Schooling (School 15, Middle school 15, High school 20, University 20) and from the Libraries covering the Home (6 to 30 over five Tiers), combined multiplicatively. It is never stored. The city's mean, weighted by Citizens, raises the output of Industry and Office buildings, Tax, Well-being, and lowers Waste, power demand and Climate emissions. Under Hard only, a Home with Knowledge below 50 is sanctioned like any other required service, through a Tax reduction, with no extra Well-being malus and none during an Adaptation period.

## Why

- Production and Office output follow the city-wide mean, not the Knowledge of the Homes whose Commuters fill the Jobs. The local version needs the Commute traced per Home and invalidates caches on every road change for little gain in play.
- Knowledge only rewards below Hard, as Esteem and Fulfilment do, so Cozy, Easy and Medium cities never lose Tax to it.
- The threshold of 50 lets a city with the four Schooling degrees (70) skip the Library on Hard, so the Library stays a way to cover missing degrees, not a hidden requirement.
- A direct Tax bonus is kept small (+5%), because Well-being already modulates Tax and the Knowledge Well-being bonus would otherwise count twice.
- Storing Knowledge per Home was rejected: it is derived from coverage like Well-being, so existing saves need no migration.

## Consequences

- The Education Need factor keeps meaning School coverage; a new Knowledge factor joins the Esteem level.
- A Library grows its footprint with its Tier (2x2, 3x2, 4x2), as the Arcade, Casino and Office already do.
- Cities already on Hard get an Adaptation period for the Knowledge Sanction.
