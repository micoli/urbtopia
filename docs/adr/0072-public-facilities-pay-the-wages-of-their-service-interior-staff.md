# A Public facility pays the wages of the Staff of its Service interior

Amends the rule that a Public facility has no operating cost, which the glossary stated and [ADR 0070](0070-interiors-come-in-three-families-venue-production-and-service.md) puts under strain.

## Decisions

- A Public facility still has no operating cost of its own, except the Waste facilities for the Waste they process.
- The Staff the player hires in a Service interior (teacher, librarian, nurse, doctor, surgeon, [ADR 0071](0071-an-interior-role-carries-its-own-part-of-the-effect.md)) cost a hiring fee and a daily wage. That wage is the only recurring cost a School, a Library or a Hospital can have.
- Hiring is voluntary. With no interior, or no Staff hired, the facility costs nothing and serves as before, so existing saves and balance do not move.

## Why

A wage that did not cost anything would make the Staff free value. Keeping it optional keeps the Public facilities' rule intact for a player who never opens the interior.

## Considered options

- **Wages paid by the city's Tax budget.** Rejected: there is no such budget today.
- **Free Staff.** Rejected: there would be no trade-off in hiring.

## Consequences

- The glossary entry of "Public facility" now names these wages.
- What happens to a facility whose wages cannot be paid is still to be decided with the Service interior itself.
