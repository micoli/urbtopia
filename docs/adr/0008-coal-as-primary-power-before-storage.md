# Coal as primary power before storage

The player must be able to choose an affordable, viable polluting energy policy instead of being restricted to renewable generation with expensive backup. Coal Power plants provide stable generation after renewables but before neighborhood batteries; the existing deficit-only backup remains after batteries. Fuel is abstracted into operating costs because the stored coal Material unlocks too late to support this early-game choice.

This extends ADR-0005's allocation sequence: coal preserves stored renewable energy by serving current Demand first, at the cost of ongoing expenditure, emissions and bounded local well-being penalties. Neither coal nor backup charges batteries. Existing wind and backup buildings retain their identities and behavior, avoiding an involuntary conversion of saved cities.
