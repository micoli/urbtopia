# Arenas earn only from their Gatherings

Attractions used to earn twice: every one drew Tourists all day, and the Theater, the Concert hall, the Colosseum and the stadiums added Gatherings on top. A concert hall or a stadium is not a place Tourists come to see. We split them: an Attraction is seen all day and holds no Gathering; an Arena (Theater, Concert hall, stadium) earns only from its Gatherings, and with none it draws nobody and earns nothing. The Colosseum is the one deliberate exception, both an Attraction and an Arena, because it is a monument that also stages Shows.

A stadium with no Club holds a free Meeting every day, as the athletics stadium already did. A Meeting seats 600 people whatever the stadium; only a Match fills the whole stadium.

## Why

- Without Meetings, football and baseball stadiums would earn nothing in production, where Clubs sit behind the `clubs` flag. Keeping their all-day Tourists until Clubs ship was rejected: it would leave two rules for the same building.
- Meetings at full capacity were rejected: a football stadium would go from about 2,340 to 11,700 Urbs a day and pay back its 25,000 Urbs in about two days, against the two to three weeks of a landmark. At 600 seats every stadium earns at most about 2,340 Urbs a day, and founding a Club stays the reward for filling it.
- An agenda of several Shows was rejected for now: it changes the save format, the payment and the cooldown, and its forecasts would go stale. A Concert hall left without a Show therefore earns nothing, which is what makes programming matter.

## Consequences

- An Arena's Takings have no cap, since the cap bounds an all-day flow it no longer has. The Colosseum keeps its cap for visits. Takings already held in a save stay collectable.
- Arenas still count towards `cityAttractiveness`, so existing cities do not lose Tourists elsewhere.
- The Championship boost no longer raises all-day Tourists at a stadium: it applies to the crowd of the next Match.
- The Colosseum has one Ticket for its visits and its Shows.
