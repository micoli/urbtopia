# Independent transit networks and purchased fleets

Ordinary Roads, BRT corridors and Railways remain independent networks: perpendicular crossings preserve each network's continuity without permitting vehicles to switch networks. Railway propulsion belongs to purchased Trains rather than tracks, allowing electric and coal Trains to share a line; purchased BRT vehicles are electric. This separates infrastructure investment from fleet choices and avoids treating dedicated transit as an ordinary Road variant, at the cost of explicit network-aware placement, crossings and routing.
