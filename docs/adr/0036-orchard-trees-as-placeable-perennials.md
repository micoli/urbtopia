# Orchard trees are placeable perennial game objects

Orchard trees are placed on free tiles in owned parcels, cost Urbs, need water, and do not require Fields, Seed packs or road access. Each tree produces one fruit at a time for manual Harvest, then remains in place and starts another production cycle. Ready fruit is lost if the tree is removed.

The fruit Materials use the crops storage compartment. Orchard trees also contribute cooling, biodiversity and wellbeing through the existing green-space benefits, including the climate system's temperature mitigation.

## Why

- Trees should be freely placed city objects rather than another Crop: Crops occupy Field tiles, consume Seed packs and are removed from their Field on Harvest.
- Keeping trees as perennial objects lets them produce repeatedly without changing the existing Crop harvest lifecycle.
- Reusing green-space benefits gives trees a local ecological effect that contributes to the city's cooling without adding a separate climate rule.

## Consequences

- Orchard trees have their own placement, production, water demand, harvesting and ecological behavior while their fruit remains a Material.
- The Mill produces Flour from Wheat and Olive oil from Olives. The Bakery produces Bread from Flour, Fougasse from Flour and Olive oil, Apple cake from Flour and Apples, and Cherry clafoutis from Flour and Cherries. Each recipe consumes one of each input and produces one Food Good.
- Trees take 20 game minutes per fruit and each orchard tree adds one unit of water demand. Olive and apple trees unlock at 20 Citizens; cherry trees and the fruit recipes unlock at 80. The Mill and Bakery use the existing five-tier production balance.

## Alternatives rejected

- Make trees Crops on Fields: this would require Seed packs and would remove the tree on Harvest, conflicting with free placement and repeat production.
- Make each tree a Workshop: trees produce passively and are harvested directly rather than queued and collected as building production.
