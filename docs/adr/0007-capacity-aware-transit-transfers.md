# Capacity-aware transit transfers

Public transport supports itineraries across ordinary Bus lines, BRT lines and Railway lines, with Transfers between stops within two tiles and at most two Transfers per itinerary. This extends ADR 0005's coverage-based mobility to Homes and activities served by different lines: choose the fastest feasible itinerary, consume capacity on every used line and deduplicate citywide riders. Transfers are included in the initial release rather than postponed, while individual Citizen journeys remain unsimulated to bound simulation and Catch-up costs.
