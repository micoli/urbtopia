# Promo videos

`node scripts/promo/cli.ts <scenario> [--fresh] [--scene=<name>]` films `scripts/promo/scenarios/<scenario>.promo.ts` against the running dev server (`PROMO_URL`, default `http://127.0.0.1:5175`) and writes `tmp/promo/<scenario>.mp4`. `npm run promo -- <scenario>` does the same.

- A scene is `scene(name, { state, language, time, viewport, ui, hideTaxBadges }, async (stage) => { ... })`. `state` is a save file path from the repository root (`saves/ideal-city.json`). `hideTaxBadges: true` hides the tax bubbles over the buildings (the `showTaxBadges` preference).
- Verbs of `Stage`: `wait`, `caption`, `hideCaption`, `move`, `click`, `point(target)`, `circle(target)` (the cursor rests on, or loops around, a widget to show it), `clickAt`, `clickTile(x, y)`, `type`, `drag`, `zoom`, `flyTo(x, z, zoom, seconds)`, `timelapse({ hours, seconds })`, `skip(hours)`, `send(command)`, `pinCycleHour`.
- Scenes are cached in `tmp/promo/<scenario>/` by hash of the scene, its state and `scripts/promo/*.ts`; `--fresh` films again. `--format=phone` films the whole scenario again in portrait (720×1280, `tmp/promo/<scenario>.phone.mp4`, its own cache folder); `landscape` (1080×768) is the default; a scene `viewport` option wins over the format. In a portrait format the zooms are scaled by the width ratio (`stage.portrait` tells the format) and `stage.shiftRoom(dx, dy)` moves the open interior room on the screen. `--scene=<name>` films one scene and does not assemble. `--fast` runs the scenario to test it (no pause, no video, no assembly, `stage.cinematic` blocks skipped, `stage.check` kept); `PROMO_TRACE=1` prints the time of each verb.
- `?promo` (dev only) hides the banners, hints, explanatory notes, tooltips and guided demonstrations, and the DEV button, shows a fake cursor and the captions, and lights the map as Daytime outside a time-lapse.
- The interface is hidden by default. A scene lists the parts it shows (`ui: ['hud']`, then `stage.ui('hud', 'build')` as the action moves); the main menu (the dock buttons) is never hidden: `hud` (Urbs, clock, stats), `camera` (undo, redo, layout buttons), `build` (Build menu and confirm pad), `selection` (the floating bar of a selected building), `panel` (the side panel), `analytics` (the legend, flow lists and readout of an Analytics layer, hidden by default: `stage.analytics('movement.cars')` opens a layer by code). A part a click needs must be listed before the click.
- The game is filmed in layout C (side panel), with the pedestrians drawn as the players' avatars (`pedestrianModel: 'avatar'`), never as stick figures. The Build menu row opens the Codex on its name; a building is chosen with the picture/price button (`.flyout-row .flyout-info`). A click on a free tile places the building at once, after the mouse has moved over it.
- Interiors: `scripts/promo/interior.ts` has `openInteriorBuild(stage)` and `placeFixture(stage, { section, fixture, cell, rotations })`. A row of a build menu carries `data-choice="<id>"` (the Fixture id), whatever the language; the interior menu closes after each placement, `placeFixture` reopens it. `tileToScreen` and `clickTile` use the cells of the open interior. `scripts/promo/states/early-city.json` (60 Citizens, 6000 U) is built by `node scripts/promo/buildEarlyCity.ts`. A scene that fails leaves `tmp/promo/<scenario>/<scene>.error.png`.
- The `promo-video` skill drives all this from a scenario described by the user.

## Voice over and effects

- `stage.say(text, { wait, speed })` speaks a line with Kokoro (Kokoro-FastAPI in Docker, `KOKORO_URL`, default `http://localhost:8880`; started as the container `urbtopia-kokoro` from `ghcr.io/remsky/kokoro-fastapi-cpu:v0.10.0` when nothing answers). Lines are cached in `tmp/promo/voice/`. The scene option `voice` picks the voice (`af_heart` for `en`, `ff_siwis` for `fr`). The cues are stored with the scene and mixed at assembly over a silent base; `--fast` does not speak.
- Effects of the page: `title`, `caption(text, ms, mode, wordMs)` (default mode `'viral'`: uppercase outlined words, three at a time, each popping in with a yellow highlight; also `'words'`, `'typewriter'`, `'plain'`), `callout` / `clearCallout`, `stat`, and a light vignette. Between scenes the assembly fades through black; `scenario(name, scenes, { transition: 'cut', audio })` changes it and adds a music bed.

## Languages, music and floating menus

- A scenario is `scenario(name, (language) => scenes, options)` and every video is filmed in English and French (`--lang=all` by default): `tmp/promo/<scenario>.en.mp4`, `.fr.mp4`, with `.phone` before `.mp4` for portrait. The language of the game, of the captions and of the voice (`af_heart` / `ff_siwis`) follow it. `stage.label('<message key>')` reads a label of the game in that language, so selectors never hold a text.
- Music: `scripts/promo/assets/mythica.mp3` ("Mythica", congusbongus, CC0, https://opengameart.org/content/mythica) is mixed under the voice by default (volume 0.16, fades) and credited on the closing card (`scripts/promo/credits.ts`).
- Act through the floating menus (the `selection` part of `ui`): the floating bar of a building and the toolbar of an interior fixture; side panels only for what they alone offer.

## Driving the game by code (no mouse, no floating bar)

- `stage.analytics('movement.cars')` / `stage.analytics(null)` open and leave a Layer of the Analytics view; its legend, flow lists and readout are hidden unless a scene lists the `analytics` part of `ui`.
- `stage.management('eco-needs' | 'eco-emissions' | 'eco-temperature' | 'eco-nature'…)` / `stage.management(null)` open City management on a section. Its code loads on first use (about 4 s): open it once with `preload` behind a `stage.title(…, { during })` card.
- `stage.interior('factory')` / `stage.interior(null)` open and close the interior of the first building of a type; `stage.cursor(false)` hides the fake cursor for a shot that shows the game alone. Fixtures go in with the `PlaceInteriorFixture` command (rotation 0 faces the front of the room, 1 the left, 2 the back wall, 3 the right); a Factory room holds a full set-up (plumbing, motor lines with bevel gears, every Machine) from Tier 5.
- `stage.level(-2)` goes down to a metro level, `stage.rotateView(1 | -1)` turns the view a quarter turn: two angles on the same place.
- A scene option `flags: ['clubs']` switches on a feature that ships switched off (Clubs, for a stadium).
- A viral text appears once per video and language (`stage.caption` skips one already shown); a text asked twice in a row is not shown again. The badges over the buildings (collect, taxes) are always hidden.
- A long scenario is a class with one private method per sequence and a `play()` that lists them in order (`scenarios/start.promo.ts`): reordering sequences is moving a line. `scripts/promo/buildSmallTown.ts` builds its start state.
